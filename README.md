## Curriculum

### Foundations and History of Journalism

This subject covers the democratic purpose, constitutional role, historical evolution, and core operating principles of journalism as the Fourth Estate.

[The Elements of Journalism: What Newspeople Should Know and the Public Should Expect (Bill Kovach & Tom Rosenstiel)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9780593239353) - The primary theoretical text defining the democratic mandate, truth obligations, and core principles of the profession.

[A History of News (Mitchell Stephens)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9780195189919) - A historical companion to Kovach and Rosenstiel that traces how news gathering, dissemination, and public communication evolved from oral tradition to modern mass media.

[What Is News? (Coursera / Michigan State University)](https://www.coursera.org/learn/what-is-news) - A structured video course introducing fundamental news values, audience trust, and reporting functions in modern media ecosystems.

### News Writing and Reporting

This subject covers the core mechanics of newswriting, including the inverted pyramid structure, lead writing, beat reporting, and objective deadline drafting.

[Inside Reporting: A Practical Guide to the Craft of Journalism (Tim Harrower)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9780073526171) - The foundational visual textbook for mastering story architecture, hard-news leads, and beat coverage routines.

[Become a Journalist: Report the News! Specialization (Coursera / Michigan State University)](https://www.coursera.org/specializations/become-a-journalist) - An applied video sequence designed to be completed alongside Harrower, providing hands-on reporting assignments and structured peer reviews.

### Interviewing, Observation, and Sourcing

This subject covers human source discovery, question design, interview psychology, field observation, and source management ethics.

[Writing and Reporting News: A Coaching Method (Carole Rich)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9781305077331) - The primary practical text using real-world newsroom examples to coach reporters on source development, note-taking, and listening techniques.

[The Craft of Interviewing (John Brady)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9780394724690) - A specialized companion to Rich that provides tactical guidance on handling reluctant subjects, off-the-record agreements, and conversational dynamics.

[Gathering and Developing the News (Coursera / Michigan State University)](https://www.coursera.org/learn/gather-develop-news) - An interactive video course that provides structured exercises for identifying source networks and conducting preliminary interviews.

### Copy Editing and News Style

This subject covers line editing, grammatical precision, Associated Press style guidelines, headline writing, and newsroom editorial standards.

[The Art of Editing in the Age of Convergence (Brian S. Brooks & James L. Pinson)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9780367820107) - The standard newsroom textbook teaching macro- and micro-editing, headline packaging, cutline formulation, and desk editorial judgment.

[Good with Words: Writing and Editing Specialization (Coursera / University of Michigan)](https://www.coursera.org/specializations/good-with-words-writing-and-editing) - A practical video companion to Brooks and Pinson focusing specifically on syntax flow, sentence-level concision, and systematic self-editing.

### Verification and Fact-Checking

This subject covers digital content verification, image and video forensics, geolocation, source authentication, and open-source intelligence methods.

[Verification Handbook: A Definitive Guide to Verifying Digital Content for Emergency Coverage (Craig Silverman, ed., European Journalism Centre)](https://datajournalism.com/read/handbook/verification-1) - The open-access field manual detailing professional workflows for authenticating user-generated media and real-time eyewitness claims.

[Verification: The Basics (European Journalism Centre / DataJournalism.com)](https://datajournalism.com/watch/verification-the-basics) - A companion video course taught by the handbook's authors that demonstrates practical tools for geolocation, reverse image search, and digital provenance checks.

[Crash Course: Navigating Digital Information (YouTube / CrashCourse & SHEG)](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PL8dPuuaLjXtN07XYqqWSKpPrtNDiCHTzU) - A fast-paced video series teaching lateral reading, click restraint, and evidentiary evaluation developed in partnership with the Stanford History Education Group.

### Media Law and Freedom of Expression

This subject covers legal boundaries governing the press, including defamation and libel standards, invasion of privacy, reporter's privilege, copyright, and freedom of information laws.

[Mass Media Law (Clay Calvert, Dan V. Kozlowski, & Derigan Silver)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9781260837421) - The comprehensive academic textbook covering essential legal doctrines, First Amendment jurisprudence, and digital publishing liability.

[The First Amendment Handbook (Reporters Committee for Freedom of the Press)](https://www.rcfp.org/resources/first-amendment-handbook/) - A free, practical field manual that translates legal doctrine into immediate guidance on resisting subpoenas, protecting sources, and asserting public access rights.

### Media Ethics and Professional Standards

This subject covers moral decision-making frameworks, conflict-of-interest management, harm minimization, anonymous sourcing, and newsroom accountability.

[Doing Ethics in Media: Theories and Practical Applications (Jay Black & Chris Roberts)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9781138041110) - The core textbook applying classical moral philosophies and a structured decision-making model to contemporary newsroom dilemmas.

[Media Ethics & Governance (Coursera / University of Amsterdam)](https://www.coursera.org/learn/media-ethics-governance) - An online lecture course that complements Black and Roberts by examining media accountability systems, institutional regulation, and moral reasoning.

### Investigative Reporting and Public Records

This subject covers hypothesis-driven investigative inquiry, statutory public records retrieval (FOIA), document trails, court docket analysis, and source security.

[Story-Based Inquiry: A Manual for Investigative Journalists (Mark Lee Hunter et al., UNESCO)](https://unesdoc.unesco.org/ark:/48223/pf0000193078) - The foundational open-access manual that outlines how to organize long-term investigations through hypotheses, timelines, and structured evidence matrices.

[The Investigative Reporter's Handbook: A Guide to Documents, Databases, and Techniques (Brant Houston & Investigative Reporters and Editors)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9781319102845) - A detailed operational companion to Hunter that shows how to systematically locate, decode, and analyze public filings, court transcripts, and corporate databases.

### Digital Storytelling and Social Media Reporting

This subject covers web-native journalism formats, live coverage, social newsgathering, mobile reporting workflows, and audience engagement strategies.

[Journalism Next: A Practical Guide to Digital Innovation (Mark Briggs)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9781544309446) - The core handbook covering the technological shifts, digital workflows, and publishing formats required in modern digital newsrooms.

[Reuters Digital Journalism Course (Reuters & Meta Journalism Project)](https://reutersdigitaljournalism.com/) - A free, self-paced certification course designed to reinforce Briggs through interactive modules on mobile reporting, online newsgathering, and social curation.

### Multimedia Journalism (Audio and Visual Storytelling)

This subject covers visual framing, news photojournalism, digital video production, field audio capture, podcast development, and multiplatform story packaging.

[Multimedia Journalism: A Practical Guide (Andy Bull)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9781138725911) - The comprehensive technical textbook detailing how to conceptualize, shoot, record, and assemble cross-platform visual and digital packages.

[Sound Reporting: The NPR Guide to Audio Journalism and Production (Jonathan Kern)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9780226431789) - A specialized companion to Bull focusing strictly on the audio medium, covering ambient sound recording, voice-track writing, and audio narrative structure.

### Data Journalism and Numeracy

This subject covers newsroom math, spreadsheet analysis, dataset hygiene, rate calculations, and introductory data visualization for news stories.

[The Data Journalism Handbook (Jonathan Gray, Liliana Bounegru, & Lucy Chambers, eds., European Journalism Centre)](https://datajournalism.com/read/handbook/one) - An open-access guide detailing real-world data reporting case studies, source extraction methods, and newsroom workflows from international investigative teams.

[Doing Journalism with Data: First Steps, Skills and Tools (European Journalism Centre / DataJournalism.com)](https://datajournalism.com/watch/doing-journalism-with-data-first-steps-skills-and-tools) - A structured video course providing hands-on instruction in spreadsheet formulas, pivot tables, data cleaning, and basic news charting tools.
