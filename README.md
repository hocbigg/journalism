# Summary

This roadmap provides a structured, self-paced curriculum equivalent to a 4-year undergraduate degree in Journalism.

## Organization

This repository is organized into three main components:

- **Core Curriculum** (this page): the foundational knowledge of the field;
- **[Advanced Topics](advanced_topics.md)**: focused study in specific areas;
- **[Projects](projects.md)**: support learning through practical application throughout the curriculum.

**Process:** Learners may work through the curriculum independently or collaboratively, and either sequentially or selectively.

- For simplicity, courses in the Core Curriculum are ordered according to their prerequisites.
- The Core Curriculum provides a shared foundation and is intended to be completed in full.
- Advanced Topics are optional; learners are encouraged to select one area of focus and complete all courses within that topic.

Practical work is integrated through the [Projects section](projects.md) and may be undertaken alongside coursework.

Note: When there are courses or books that don't fit into the curriculum but are otherwise of high quality,
they belong in [extras/courses](extras/courses.md), [extras/readings](extras/readings.md).

## Orientation and Professional Foundations

| Subject | Why study? | Book | Videos |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Introduction to Journalism | Establishes the purpose, values, democratic role, and foundational theories of the fourth estate. | [The Elements of Journalism: What Newspeople Should Know and the Public Should Expect](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/) (Bill Kovach & Tom Rosenstiel) | [Become a Journalist: Report the News! Specialization](https://www.coursera.org/specializations/become-a-journalist) (Coursera / Michigan State University) |
| Media Literacy and Critical Thinking | Teaches how to critically deconstruct information ecosystems, identify cognitive biases, and evaluate news reliability. | *Blur: How to Know What's True in the Age of Information Overload* (Bill Kovach & Tom Rosenstiel) | [Making Sense of the News: News Literacy for Adults](https://www.coursera.org/learn/news-literacy) (Coursera / University of Hong Kong & Stony Brook University) |
| History of Journalism and Media Systems | Contextualizes the evolution of the press, constitutional protections, technological shifts, and media ownership models. | *A History of News* (Mitchell Stephens, Oxford University Press) | [CrashCourse Media History & Media Literacy](https://thecrashcourse.com/) (CrashCourse / PBS) |

## Verification, Ethics, and Law

| Subject | Why study? | Book | Videos |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Verification and Fact-Checking | Equips students with digital forensics, geolocation, reverse image search, and source authentication workflows. | [Verification Handbook: A definitive guide to verifying digital content for emergency coverage](https://datajournalism.com/read/handbook/verification-1) (Craig Silverman, ed., European Journalism Centre) | [First Draft: Essential Verification Guides and Case Studies](https://firstdraftnews.org/) (First Draft News) |
| Media Ethics and Professional Standards | Navigates moral decision-making frameworks, conflicts of interest, harm minimization, undercover reporting, and public accountability. | [SPJ Code of Ethics](https://www.spj.org/ethics/) (Society of Professional Journalists) & *Doing Ethics in Media: Theories and Practical Applications* (Jay Black & Chris Roberts) | [Media Ethics & Governance](https://www.coursera.org/learn/media-ethics-governance) (Coursera / University of Amsterdam) |
| Media Law and Freedom of Expression | Explains defamation, libel, invasion of privacy, copyright, reporter's privilege, shield laws, and freedom of information statutes. | *Mass Media Law* (Clay Calvert, Dan V. Kozlowski, & Derigan Silver, McGraw-Hill) & [The First Amendment Handbook](https://www.rcfp.org/resources/first-amendment-handbook/) (Reporters Committee for Freedom of the Press) | [Reporters Committee Legal Training Series](https://www.rcfp.org/) (Reporters Committee for Freedom of the Press) |
| Journalist Safety, Security, and Resilience | Prepares reporters for physical field safety, digital operational security, situational awareness, and psychological resilience. | [CPJ Journalist Safety Guide](https://cpj.org/security/guide/) (Committee to Protect Journalists) | [CPJ Safety Kit and Training Resources](https://cpj.org/safety-kit/) (Committee to Protect Journalists) |
