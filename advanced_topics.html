Advanced Topics in Journalism

These specialization tracks are designed for learners who have completed foundational training in reporting, media law, and verification, and are ready to pursue advanced methodological or subfield mastery. Learners are encouraged to select one or two tracks aligned with their investigative goals and medium of focus, rather than attempting to complete every track:

  • Investigative and Forensic Journalism: Teaches digital open-source intelligence (OSINT), forensic accounting, cross-border investigative consortia, and operational security for source protection.
  • Computational and Data Journalism: Explores algorithmic accountability, programmatic web scraping in Python, reproducible statistical inference, and geospatial satellite investigation.
  • Narrative Nonfiction, Audio, and Documentary Storytelling: Examines structural narrative arcs in literary nonfiction, serialized investigative audio design, long-form documentary direction, and trauma-informed interviewing.
  • Global Journalism, Geopolitics, and Crisis Reporting: Focuses on hostile-environment conflict reporting, comparative global media systems, investigating state disinformation campaigns, and environmental enterprise reporting.
  • News Innovation, Media Economics, and Platform Governance: Analyzes commercial media economics, newsroom product management, tech platform distribution power, and generative AI newsroom governance.

Investigative and Forensic Journalism

This track covers advanced investigative methodologies for exposing transnational crime, corporate malfeasance, state abuse, and institutional corruption.

Open-Source Intelligence (OSINT) and Digital Forensics: Berkeley Protocol on Digital Open Source Investigations (UN OHCHR & UC Berkeley Human Rights Center) - Establishes international legal and technical standards for identifying, collecting, verifying, and preserving digital open-source information, social media evidence, and satellite imagery for investigative accountability.

Financial Forensics and Corporate Investigations: The Investigative Journalist's Guide to Company Accounts (Raj Bairoliya / Centre for Investigative Journalism) - A practical open handbook teaching reporters how to read corporate balance sheets, audit profit-and-loss statements, trace shell companies, and detect accounting maneuvers used to conceal fraud.

Cross-Border Collaborative Investigations: Cross-Border Collaborative Journalism: A Step-by-Step Guide (Brigitte Alfter / Routledge) - The foundational methodology manual on organizing transnational reporting consortia, managing secure shared data repositories, navigating cross-cultural editorial workflows, and synchronizing cross-border publications.

Whistleblower Protection, Encryption, and Source OpSec: Information Security for Journalists (Silkie Carlo & Arjen Kamphuis / Centre for Investigative Journalism) - A comprehensive practitioner guide to threat modeling, hardware security, Tails OS, disk encryption, and secure communications protocols required to protect high-risk sources and whistleblowers.

Computational and Data Journalism

This track focuses on advanced computational techniques, programming environments, and statistical rigor for uncovering stories within complex codebases and massive datasets.

Algorithmic Accountability and AI Auditing: Automating the News: How Algorithms Are Rewriting the Media (Nicholas Diakopoulos / Harvard University Press) - The definitive academic text exploring how to investigate algorithmic decision-making, scoring systems, automated news processes, and computational accountability in public life.

Algorithmic Accountability and AI Auditing: Algorithmic Accountability Reporting: On the Investigation of Black Boxes (Nicholas Diakopoulos / Tow Center for Digital Journalism) - A companion open-access research guide detailing reverse-engineering methods, input-output auditing frameworks, and investigative approaches for interrogating opaque proprietary algorithms.

Programmatic Data Extraction and Web Scraping: Python for Journalists (Winny de Jong / DataJournalism.com) - A structured, free video course that walks reporters through using Jupyter Notebooks, writing automated Python scrapers, and cleaning unstructured text datasets using Pandas.

Statistical Inference and Precision Journalism: The Chicago Guide to Writing about Numbers (Jane E. Miller / University of Chicago Press) - An authoritative guide to interpreting distributions, regression models, percentage changes, and confidence intervals to ensure quantitative assertions are analytically sound and clearly written.

Geospatial Investigation and Remote Sensing: Resources for Finding and Using Satellite Images (Global Investigative Journalism Network) - A comprehensive practitioner guide and tool inventory detailing how to access optical and radar satellite imagery, utilize Google Earth Engine, and apply remote sensing to environmental and human rights investigations.

Narrative Nonfiction, Audio, and Documentary Storytelling

This track examines long-form narrative craft across prose, serialized audio, and documentary film, emphasizing character development, dramatic structure, and ethical immersion.

Literary Journalism and Narrative Architecture: Telling True Stories: A Nonfiction Writers' Guide from the Nieman Foundation at Harvard University (Mark Kramer & Wendy Call, eds. / Plume) - The benchmark anthology of craft essays by leading narrative journalists, breaking down field reporting immersion, scene reconstruction, voice, pacing, and narrative ethics.

Long-Form Serial Audio and Sound Design: Reality Radio: Telling True Stories in Sound (John Biewen & Alexa Dilworth, eds. / University of North Carolina Press) - An essential exploration of the documentary audio craft, analyzing acoustic scene-building, multi-track narrative scoring, archival integration, and voice-track writing for serial podcasts.

Investigative Documentary Filmmaking: Directing the Documentary (Michael Rabiger & Courtney Hermann / Routledge) - The canonical master text on long-form visual dramaturgy, cinéma vérité ethics, interviewing techniques for camera, archival clearances, and structural post-production editing.

Trauma-Informed Interviewing and Sensitive Reporting: Dart Center Style Guide for Trauma-Informed Journalism (Dart Center for Journalism and Trauma / Columbia Journalism School) - A peer-reviewed open reference guide outlining ethical protocols for interviewing survivors of violence, minimizing re-traumatization, and accurately reporting on psychological trauma.

Global Journalism, Geopolitics, and Crisis Reporting

This track prepares reporters to navigate complex international media ecosystems, armed conflicts, coordinated influence operations, and cross-border environmental crises.

War Correspondence and Conflict Zone Reporting: Safety Guide for Journalists: A Handbook for Reporters in High-Risk Environments (Reporters Without Borders & UNESCO) - A comprehensive operational field manual covering pre-assignment risk assessments, hostile environment security, battlefield medical preparedness, digital security in conflict zones, and psychological self-care.

Comparative Media Systems and Press Freedom: Comparing Media Systems: Three Models of Media and Politics (Daniel C. Hallin & Paolo Mancini / Cambridge University Press) - The foundational comparative framework analyzing the relationships between political systems, media market structures, journalistic professionalism, and state intervention across democracies.

State-Sponsored Disinformation and Information Warfare: Verification Handbook for Disinformation and Media Manipulation (Craig Silverman, ed. / European Journalism Centre) - A free, open-access field guide detailing practical forensic methodologies for tracking coordinated inauthentic behavior, botnet networks, state-backed influence operations, and manipulated synthetic media.

Transnational Environmental and Climate Investigation: Covering the Environment: How Journalists Work the Green Beat (Bob Wyss / Routledge) - A comprehensive guide detailing how to investigate environmental regulatory agencies, interpret climate and atmospheric data, untangle resource extraction supply chains, and expose environmental pollution.

News Innovation, Media Economics, and Platform Governance

This track examines the structural, commercial, and technological forces reshaping the news industry, from subscription economics to platform gatekeeping and artificial intelligence.

Media Economics, Reader Revenue, and Sustainable Business Models: The Economics and Financing of Media Companies (Robert G. Picard / Fordham University Press) - The standard economic analysis of media firm capital structures, multi-sided markets, advertising elasticity, audience valuation, and emerging reader-revenue architectures.

Newsroom Product Management and Audience Development: Product Strategies for Journalism: How to Align Editorial, Audience, Business and Technology (Adriana Lacy / Knight Center for Journalism in the Americas) - A free, four-module professional course created with the News Product Alliance that teaches agile workflows, audience conversion funnels, product thinking, and cross-functional newsroom collaboration.

Platform Power, Algorithmic Intermediation, and Media Policy: The Platform Society: Public Values in a Connective World (José van Dijck, Thomas Poell, & Martijn de Waal / Oxford University Press) - The seminal scholarly text dissecting platformization, algorithmic curation, infrastructural dependence, and regulatory struggles between Big Tech and news organizations.

Generative AI, Newsroom Integration, and Synthetic Media Ethics: Generating Change: A Global Survey of What News Organisations Are Doing with AI (Charlie Beckett & Mira Yaseen / Polis, London School of Economics) - An exhaustive global research report analyzing data from 105 newsrooms across 46 countries, outlining practical applications, workflow shifts, algorithmic risks, and editorial governance models for AI in journalism.
